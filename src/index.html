CONNECT YOUR BROWSER

Bring your Chrome tabs here

Checking connection…

  1. Enable the local bridge

    This lets the extension communicate with this app.

  2. Add the Chrome extension

    Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Select the folder below.

  3. Connect a profile

    Click the extension icon in Chrome, or wait up to 30 seconds. Repeat step 2 for other profiles.
YOUR CLEANUP RULES

Preferences

Protected tabs stay open when closing individually or in bulk.

Recovery records contain URLs and titles, stored only on this computer. Grouped tabs are always excluded from suggested cleanup.
REVIEW BEFORE CLOSING

Suggested cleanup

Exact URLs only. Protected and grouped tabs stay open. Uncheck anything you want to keep. This review covers the entire connected workspace.
A WAY BACK

Recently closed

Reopens URLs in their original profile. Unsaved page state and previous group membership cannot be restored. Older sessions reopen in an available window.

Delete recovery records?

This removes the local URL history kept by TabArrange. Open Chrome tabs will not change.

Move tabs?

A new tab will be left behind if a source window would become empty.

Close window?

This closes every tab in this window, including active, pinned, and audio tabs. Unsaved work may be lost. Recovery can reopen saved URLs, but cannot restore page state.
